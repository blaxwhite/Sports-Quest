THE WORLD OF SPORT
01 · GET TO KNOW SPORT

Start with the basics

Explore these four topics. Each one introduces useful knowledge and vocabulary for the activities in the Arcade Zone.

🏅
TOPIC 01

Types of Sports

Sports are physical activities that help people stay active and healthy. They can be played alone or with a team.

  • Team sports · Football, basketball, volleyball and netball.
  • Individual sports · Swimming, gymnastics and cycling.
  • Racket sports · Badminton, tennis and squash.
  • Athletics · Running, jumping and throwing events.
🎒
TOPIC 02

Sports Equipment

Equipment refers to the items or gear needed to play a sport. Some items are used to play, while others keep athletes safe.

  • Football · Ball, goalpost and football boots.
  • Badminton · Racket, shuttlecock and net.
  • Basketball · Ball, hoop and basketball shoes.
  • Cycling · Bicycle and helmet.
  • Swimming · Swimsuit and goggles.
🏃

TOPIC 04

Sports Events

Sports events need planning. When you read or listen to an announcement, look for these important details.

EVENTWhat activity is happening?
DATE & TIMEWhen will it happen?
VENUEWhere will it take place?
PARTICIPANTSWho can take part?
WHAT TO BRINGWhat equipment is needed?
RULESWhat should participants follow?
🤝

Champion Mindset: Teamwork & Fair Play

A good sport follows the rules, respects teammates and opponents, takes turns and communicates politely. Encourage others, accept winning and losing, and always play fairly. Being a champion is about how you play, not only whether you win.

02 · BUILD YOUR VOCABULARY

Words every champion should know

Remember the meaning and example of each word. You may meet them in the reading, listening and teamwork quizzes.

OpponentA person or team you compete against.We respect our opponents.
TeammateA person who plays on your team.I pass the ball to my teammate.
RefereeA person who makes sure players follow the rules.The referee blows the whistle.
EquipmentThe items needed to play a sport.A racket is badminton equipment.
VenueThe place where an event happens.The event is at the school field.
TrainingPractice to improve a skill or performance.Our team has football training.
