THE WORLD OF SPORT
YOUR SPORTS KNOWLEDGE GUIDE

What should every champion know?

Read each topic and remember the key words. They will help you understand the questions and activities in the Arcade Zone.

🏅

1. Types of Sports

Sports are physical activities that help people stay active, healthy and strong. Some sports are played individually, while others are played in teams.

  • Team sports: football, basketball, volleyball and netball.
  • Individual sports: swimming, gymnastics, cycling and badminton singles.
  • Racket sports: badminton, tennis and squash.
  • Athletics: running, jumping and throwing events.
🎒

2. Sports Equipment

Equipment is the item or gear used to play a sport. Some equipment is used to play, while other items help protect athletes.

  • Football: ball, goalpost and football boots.
  • Badminton: racket, shuttlecock and net.
  • Basketball: ball, hoop and basketball shoes.
  • Cycling: bicycle and helmet.
  • Swimming: swimsuit and goggles.
🏃

3. Actions in Sports

Sports use action words to describe how players move and compete. Learn these verbs so you can explain what happens during a game.

  • kick a ball · throw a ball · catch a ball
  • pass the ball to a teammate · shoot towards a goal
  • dribble while moving · hit a shuttlecock
  • run, jump, swim and cycle
📣

4. Sports Events

A sports event needs good planning. When reading or listening to an announcement, look for important details.

  • Event: What activity is taking place?
  • Date and time: When will it happen?
  • Venue: Where will it take place?
  • Participants: Who can join?
  • Items to bring: What equipment or clothing is needed?

5. Be a Champion: Teamwork and Sportsmanship

A good sport follows the rules, respects teammates and opponents, takes turns, communicates politely and accepts both winning and losing. Encourage others and play fairly. Remember: teamwork is more important than winning alone.
