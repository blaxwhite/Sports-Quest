TEACHER GUIDE

A practical classroom implementation plan using ASSURE and the CASPER visual design principles.

🎯 Learning objectives

Intended audience: Year 5 English learners studying the topic of sport. By the end of the session, pupils should be able to (1) identify at least 8/10 sports vocabulary items in a quiz, (2) associate sports with appropriate equipment and actions, and (3) write three meaningful sentences using sports words.

These are proposed activity objectives. Teachers should confirm the exact DSKP content and learning standards for their class before use.

🧭 ASSURE classroom implementation

A · Analyse learners: Check pupils' prior sports vocabulary, English proficiency, reading needs and device access using a short oral warm-up. Provide teacher support for learners who need it.

S · State objectives: Share the three measurable learning goals before pupils start.

S · Select methods, media and materials: Use sports lesson cards, video comprehension, Arcade quizzes and the runner as guided multimedia practice.

U · Utilise media and materials: Before class, test internet access, video playback, keyboard/touch controls, sound levels and projection. Preview content. Prepare an offline alternative if the website is unavailable.

R · Require learner participation: Ask pupils to answer quiz questions, discuss a sports word with a partner, complete a game challenge and write three example sentences.

E · Evaluate and revise: Check quiz accuracy against the 8/10 target and review sentences and reflections. Re-teach misunderstood vocabulary and revise unclear instructions or difficult questions after collecting feedback.

⏱️ Suggested 35-minute lesson

0–5 min: Activate prior knowledge and explain objectives.

5–13 min: Explore Sports Guide and model vocabulary pronunciation.

13–20 min: Watch a selected video and answer comprehension questions.

20–29 min: Complete Arcade quiz and discuss mistakes.

29–35 min: Write three sports sentences and submit Champion Reflection.

The runner game may be used as optional reinforcement if time permits.

🎨 CASPER design decisions

Contrast: Gold text and buttons stand out on dark teal backgrounds.

Alignment: Lessons and Arcade choices use consistent card grids.

Simplicity: Sports Guide, Arcade, Reflection and Champions are separated by purpose.

Proximity: Related explanations, visuals and controls are grouped inside cards.

Emphasis: Large headings and prominent calls to action signal the next step.

Repetition: The same colours, typography and fantasy visual motifs appear across pages.

📝 Evaluation checklist

☐ Pupils can navigate without repeated assistance.

☐ At least 8/10 vocabulary items are identified correctly.

☐ Pupils match sports to equipment and actions.

☐ Pupils write three meaningful sports-related sentences.

☐ Video, quizzes and game controls function on the available devices.

☐ Teacher records common errors and decides what to improve.

The website's leaderboard is device-local, not a class-wide verified assessment system. The reflection page stores responses on the same browser; teachers must review them on that device if needed.
